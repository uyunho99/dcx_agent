## Choices where the design was open

- Used string Enums for the exact specified choice codes and a generic `ChoiceWithNote` specialized for project type and analysis goal.
- `targetScope` and `futureCustomer` may be omitted, null, or empty objects. Their list fields default independently to empty lists; unselected target dimensions render as `전체`.
- `ageRanges`, `genders`, and the optional research question `template` are strings because the permitted design excerpt supplies no codes for them.
- Required fields remain required, including `constraints` (which permits an empty list). `keyMetrics` and `channels` require at least one entry. No additional nonempty-string restriction was invented.
- Preserved the supplied Korean labels verbatim. Added Korean labels for codes without prescribed display wording. LABELS is a plain nested dictionary with one literal code/label pair per line; its group keys include `price`, `market`, `source`, and `alignmentSource`.
- Rendered general project/product details in 0-A and optional target/future-customer details in 0-B under the exact baseline sentence. Choice notes appear in parentheses next to their labels; the shared target-scope note has its own line.
- Used fixed field ordering, retained list input order, LF separators, and a final newline. No timestamps, external calls, or mutations are involved.
- The `known` argument is authoritative for `## 이미 아는 것`; `ctx.knownInsights` is not merged or duplicated. Empty known lists render `- 없음` beneath the header.
- Defined `AlignmentWarning` only; no warning generation or extra ProjectContext field was introduced.
- Validation covers the six mandatory tests plus invalid codes, schema version, list minimums, JSON round trip, independent defaults, enum label coverage, notes, optional rendering, and input immutability.
